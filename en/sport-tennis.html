Tennis

Tennis at Dinamika

Tennis is one of twelve sports at Hoërskool Dinamika — part of our holistic approach, in which sport enriches the social and cultural life of the school.

Contact the school for more information about practice times, coaches and matches. New players and athletes are always welcome.

Sports kit

  • Girls: school tennis dress and white socks
  • Boys: school Friday shirt, school rugby shorts and white socks

Full school uniform
