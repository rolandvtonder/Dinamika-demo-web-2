Why Dinamika?

A school that empowers learners

Parallel medium

Teaching in Afrikaans and English — a high school for the whole community.

Small classes

Class sizes range from 15 to 30 learners, so every learner gets attention.

Four pillars

Academics, discipline, Christian values and holistic development.

Wide subject choice

From Mathematics and IT to Agricultural Technology, Hospitality Studies and the arts.

Sport and culture

Twelve sports, plus choir, drama, public speaking and the eisteddfod.

Modern classrooms

A projector in every classroom and a generator that keeps classes running during load shedding.

In three steps

How to apply

  1. Grade 8: apply online with the Gauteng Department of Education (GDE).
  2. Grades 9–12: collect an internal placement application form at the school office.
  3. Hand in the completed form and the original certified documents at the school.
