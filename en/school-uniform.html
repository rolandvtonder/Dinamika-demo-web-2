School Uniform

A neat, uniform dress code promotes unity and pride. Every learner wears the uniform as an ambassador of Dinamika.

In this section
  • Overview
  • Applications
  • School Fees
  • School Uniform

At Hoërskool Dinamika we take pride in our school traditions, ethos and approachability. We are a values-based school committed to developing competent, well-rounded, polite and socially responsible young people equipped to face the future with confidence.

School unity and pride is one of the ways in which we achieve our mission. A neat, uniform dress code in all aspects of school life promotes this unity and pride.

Ties and blazers are compulsory on Tuesdays and at official school events. When no tie is worn, learners wear an open-neck shirt. Blazers may be worn on other days too, but then with a tie. Jerseys and pullovers may be worn with or without a blazer and tie.

Friday wear

Friday shirt, blue denims and white sneakers. On cold days the school jersey, tracksuit jacket and/or bomber jacket may be worn as well.

Tracksuit

School tracksuits and sneakers are worn by athletes to sports events.

Bomber jacket

May be worn with Friday wear or sports attire. This jacket is not a compulsory item.
