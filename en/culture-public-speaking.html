Public Speaking

In charge
Mrs Feenstra

Mrs Feenstra has been in charge of public speaking for the past seven years. She holds a BA(Ed) and a BEd Hons degree, which come in handy as her majors are Afrikaans and English.

The ability to speak well is an important skill everyone needs in life. That is why public speaking is not really about who wins, but about taking part.

Every year we hold an internal competition early in February, and our school also takes part in the ATKV public speaking competition. We have not made it to the national final yet, but we are working on it. As they say: perseverance wins!

Ready for the spotlight?

Contact Mrs Zelanda Vosloo, our culture organiser, or the school office to get involved.
